Start again?

A little ink. A lot of possibility.

Write or draw directly on the paper with your mouse, finger, or stylus. Pick a pen, choose a color, and make your mark.

Find your flow

Pen has a consistent line. Brush changes width with stylus pressure or drawing speed. Highlight lays down translucent ink. Eraser removes ink, not the paper. Increase Steady hand to smooth small wobbles.

Make room

Use Move or hold Space and drag. Two fingers pan and pinch the sheet. An immediate two-finger gesture cancels only its starting dot; ink already drawn is kept. Scroll to zoom; Fit brings the whole page back.

Take it with you

Export image saves PNG, JPEG, WebP (when your browser can encode it), SVG, or BMP. Transparent PNG is useful for signatures and overlays. Crop to ink removes empty margins. Guides are off in exports unless you include them.

File → Save editable project keeps the vector marks and paper settings in JSON. Image files are finished outputs, not editable project backups. This app does not recognize handwriting or turn it into typed text.

Your work, your device

Autosave uses this browser's storage. It is not cloud backup: clearing site data, private browsing, or moving to another browser can remove it. Export JSON for a durable copy. No telemetry, accounts, remote fonts, or drawing uploads.

Advanced mode

Add lines, arrows, boxes, ovals, and typed labels. Hold Shift while drawing shapes to constrain them. Pen-only ink makes fingers navigate instead of draw; it is not hardware-level palm rejection. Theme changes affect the interface, not the exported artwork.
